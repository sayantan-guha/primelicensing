Achinta Aich is a lawyer who operates where the law meets the living room. Unlike the elite advocates of the high court, Achinta finds his cases in the quiet tragedies of his own neighbourhood and the desperate pleas of the overlooked. His journey is one of constant friction: between his duty to the truth and the pressure of public outrage, and between his evolving career and the messy complexities of his personal life. Whether he is standing up to powerful dynasties or challenging class-based biases, Achinta’s arc is defined by his refusal to see a case as just a file. For him, every trial is a human equation, and every verdict is a step toward his own growth as a man navigating love, legacy, and the heavy cost of integrity.
